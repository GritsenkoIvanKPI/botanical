FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Write to us on WhatsApp and get a direct answer from our team.

Ask on WhatsApp
How do you guarantee quality?

Every batch is controlled and fully traceable: origin, batch number and production method are documented. We work only with carefully selected producers and monitor consistency from one batch to the next.

Which analyses and documents do you provide?

Depending on the product and your requirements: certificate of analysis, technical specification, SDS, origin and production information, and organic certification where applicable.

Can I receive a sample?

Yes. Samples are available so your team can evaluate materials before ordering. Send us the list of materials you are interested in and their intended application.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum volumes depend on the product, season and harvest. We work with both regular and large industrial lots. Tell us your needs and we will propose suitable terms.

How long does delivery take?

Materials in stock ship quickly across Europe and internationally. Lead times for custom production depend on the season and volume and are confirmed with each quotation.

Can you produce to our specification?

Yes. We produce essential oils, absolutes, concretes, CO₂ extracts and hydrolates to your technical brief, and source rare or seasonal ingredients through our network in France and Bulgaria.
